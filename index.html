Evaluating Capital Allocation in Mining: A 10-Year Junior Copper Developer Case Study

⛏️📊 DCF & Debt Modeling

How do you structure capital allocation, model project debt, and evaluate leverage sensitivity for a junior copper deposit transitioning into operational development? This complete 10-Year Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) & Debt Appraisal Model demonstrates how mine ramp-up profiles, debt service coverage, and equity leverage interact.

📌 Executive Summary & Core Assumptions

  • Initial Equity Outlay: $5.0M
  • Project Debt Financing: $25.0M (7.50% / 10-Yr, 1% fee)
  • Hurdle Rate (Discount): 10.00%
  • Copper / AISC Benchmark: $4.00 / $2.20 lb

📈 Model Key Performance Indicators

  • Annual Debt Service: ~$3.56M / year
  • Net Present Value (NPV): +$13.47M (@ 10%)
  • Internal Rate of Return: 43.83% IRR
  • Coverage Analysis: Robust Peak DSCR Protection
